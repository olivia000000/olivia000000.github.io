Transoral Surgical Robot

Worked on a 3mm transoral surgical tool at the PCIGITI Lab in SickKids Hospital.

N°002

ORCA Robot Hand

Assembled an open-source, highly dexterous double-jointed robotic hand at the PCIGITI Lab in SickKids Hospital.

  • 3D printed and silicone casted hand components.
  • Built tendon-actuated finger movement using metal wires and servo motors.
  • Integrated a servo-driven wrist with timing belt drive.
  • Collaborated with McMaster graduate students to test and program the hand.

Designed a high-mobility robot arm for controlled camera movement, including a custom gearbox and outer shell.
